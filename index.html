    FAQ

    Good questions.Straight answers.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes. Every job is run by a licensed master electrician, and the company carries general liability and workers' comp.

    Do you work in old rowhomes?

    All the time. Older Philadelphia homes hide knob and tube, cloth wiring and fuse boxes. We know that equipment and how to replace it without wrecking your plaster.

    Someone already looked and found nothing. Can you help?

    That's most of our calls. Troubleshooting the problem other electricians couldn't find is what we are known for.

    How much will it cost?

    Every job is different, so we give you a clear price before any work starts. No surprises on the bill.

    Can you clear a failed inspection?

    Yes. We correct the electrical work to code so the inspection passes properly.

    Do you work weekends?

    Saturdays 8am to 4pm, and the emergency line is answered around the clock.
